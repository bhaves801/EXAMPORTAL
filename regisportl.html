✅ Eligibility Criteria

Age: 17–25 years as on 31 Dec 2025

Qualification: Passed 10+2 or equivalent with PCB

Minimum Marks: 50% (General), 45% (SC/ST/OBC)

Attempts: Maximum 3 consecutive attempts

Nationality: Indian / OCI / NRI

📝 Syllabus

Physics: Class 11 & 12 NCERT topics

Chemistry: Physical, Organic, Inorganic Chemistry

Biology: Botany & Zoology (NCERT based)

Total: 720 marks, 180 Questions

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for NEXAM 2025? ▾
Visit the Registration page, click "Apply Now" and complete all 5 steps: Basic Details → Personal Details → Academic Details → Document Upload → Review & Submit. You will need a valid mobile number for OTP verification, a passport photograph, and your class 10 & 12 mark sheets.
What is the application fee and how can I pay? ▾
General/OBC: ₹1,600 | SC/ST/PwD/EWS: ₹900. Payment can be made via Net Banking, Credit/Debit Card, UPI, or Wallet. Late fee of ₹200 applies after the original deadline.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it? ▾
Click "Forgot Password" on the Login page. Enter your registered email or mobile number, receive an OTP, and set a new password. Contact helpdesk if your registered contact details have changed.
Can I edit my application after submission? ▾
Yes. A Correction Window will be open from 5 April to 10 April 2025. Only certain fields like exam centre preference and photograph can be edited. Name, DOB, and category cannot be changed.
What documents do I need to upload? ▾
Mandatory: Passport photograph (JPG, max 200KB), Signature (JPG, max 30KB), Photo ID (Aadhaar/Passport/PAN – max 1MB). Optional: Category certificate if claiming reservation.
When will Admit Cards be released? ▾
Admit Cards will be available for download from 28 April 2025. Log in with your Application Number and Date of Birth to access your Admit Card. No physical admit card will be sent by post.
How is the score calculated? What is the marking scheme? ▾
Each correct answer: +4 marks. Each wrong answer: −1 mark. Unattempted questions: 0. Total: 180 questions × 4 = 720 marks. Physics: 45Q (180M), Chemistry: 45Q (180M), Biology: 90Q (360M).
